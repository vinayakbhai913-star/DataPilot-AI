ℹ️ About DataPilot AI

Simple Business Analytics

📊 What it does

DataPilot AI analyzes uploaded business data and presents revenue, cost, profit, orders, AOV, margin, returns, product, category, region and monthly performance.

⚙️ How it works

Select a CSV or Excel file, click Analyze Data, and the file is sent to the FastAPI backend. The analysis is then displayed automatically.

🚀 Reports

After analysis you can save the dashboard as a PDF or download a real Excel workbook containing the calculated results.
